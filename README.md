# collect: water

Collect blue droplets with the jerrycan and avoid black droplets. Blue droplets add 10 points; missing a blue droplet costs one life. Catching a black droplet removes 10 points and one life. Droplets fall twice as fast and spawn three times as often as the original rate in every mode. Choose Easy (0.75× mode speed, 4 lives, 75 points per level), Med (1× mode speed, 3 lives, 100 points per level), or Hard (1.25× mode speed, 2 lives, 200 points per level). Clear all 10 levels to win: 750 points on Easy, 1,000 on Med, or 2,000 on Hard. Lose all your lives and restart the level or game.

Milestone messages scale with the difficulty: Easy at 75, 190, 380, 570, and 750 points; Med at 100, 250, 500, 750, and 1,000; Hard at 200, 500, 1,000, 1,500, and 2,000.

Reach every third level to regain one lost life, including levels 12, 15, and later in Endless Mode. Lives never go above the selected mode's starting total.

## Controls

- Move the bucket with the left/right arrow keys, A/D keys, on-screen arrows, or by dragging in the play area.
- Select the menu button or press P or Escape to pause and resume.
- Choose Endless Mode after clearing all 10 levels to keep playing.
